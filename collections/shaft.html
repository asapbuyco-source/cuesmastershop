      Collection: SHAFT

      🎲 These are SHAFTS — interchangeable front halves only. A shaft does NOT include the butt. It screws onto your existing cue.

      Each shaft fits via its joint pin (5/16 x 18, 3/8 x 10, Uni-Loc, Radial …). Check your cue's pin before ordering — or send us a photo and we match it. If you want a complete cue (butt + shaft), see the Cues collection.

      SHAFT COLLECTION

      Shafts That Change the Way Your Cue Plays

      The shaft is where the cue meets the ball — it decides how much deflection you fight, how much feel you get and how consistently you play. We stock our own handcrafted shaft technologies alongside 78 brand-name extra shafts from Action, Stealth, Predator, Lucasi, Cuetec, Katana and more — each with complete specifications so you know exactly what you are buying.

      Torrefied MapleKielwood stability with natural wood feel Hybrid Carbon InsertMaple feel with a carbon-stiffened front section
      • Complete joint and tip specifications on every product page
      • Every shaft hand-inspected before shipping
      • Not sure which shaft fits your cue? Ask us — we match joints to your butt's pin

      More shaft categories are on the way as our workshop adds them. In the meantime, read Carbon Fiber vs Maple: Which Shaft? to compare technologies.
