      Best Pool Cue Brands 2026: Predator, Cuetec, Mezz and More

      By Elite Cue · August 2026 · 7 min read

      Choosing a pool cue is one of the most personal decisions in billiards. The best pool cue brands earn their reputation over decades, and each one builds cues with a slightly different feel. Whether you are buying your first cue or upgrading to a tournament-grade model, this guide compares the leading cue makers so you can pick the brand that matches your game.

      Predator Pool Cues: The Tournament Standard

      Predator is the brand most professional players trust. Their cues are famous for low-deflection shafts, crisp hit and consistent joint construction. The Predator 9K Pool Cue combines premium ash with rosewood and abalone inlays, while the Predator 10K Pool Cue is a true top-of-the-line tournament cue. If you play league or competitive 9-ball, a Predator cue is rarely the wrong answer.

      Cuetec Cues: Carbon Fiber For Everyone

      Cuetec popularized affordable carbon fiber shafts. The Cuetec Cynergy SVB Gen II is the cue of choice for Shane Van Boening, and the Cuetec Cynergy Truewood pairs a wood-look forearm with the maintenance-free benefits of a carbon shaft. For players who want low deflection without the maple care routine, Cuetec is the strongest value in the category.

      Mezz Cues: Precision Engineering From Japan

      Mezz builds cues with some of the tightest tolerances in the industry. Japanese craftsmanship, seamless joints and excellent weight balance make models like the Mezz ACE Pool Cue, the Mezz EC9 and the Mezz CP-13 feel like extension of your arm. Mezz is especially popular with players who value quiet, solid hits.

      McDermott Cues: American Heritage And Value

      McDermott has been building cues in the USA for generations. The McDermott G-Series and GS-Series offer classic American styling, traditional maple hits and an excellent warranty program. If you want a dependable playing cue at a reasonable price, McDermott is hard to beat.

      Pechauer And Lucasi: Sleek Alternatives

      The Pechauer JP Series is known for its clean lines and flawless finish, while Lucasi Custom and Lucasi Hybrid cues bring slim, modern designs to the table. Both brands offer excellent feel and are often picked by players who want something a little different from the mainstream.

      How To Choose Between Pool Cue Brands

      • Low deflection: Predator and Cuetec lead for reduced cue ball squirt.
      • Classic feel: McDermott and Mezz deliver traditional, solid hits.
      • Carbon fiber: Cuetec Cynergy shafts require almost no maintenance.
      • Budget: McDermott G-Series and Lucasi offer the best value per dollar.
      • Pro level: Predator 10K and 9K are built for serious competition.
